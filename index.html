01

Summary

#

Teleprompter is a local-first teleprompter app. Your scripts, settings and reading progress stay on your device. The only off-device processing is advertising served by Google AdMob in the library and settings screens. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no crash-reporting SDKs.

02

Data stored on your device

#
  • Script titles, script text, reading position, favourites and per-script stage settings.
  • App settings such as speed, font size, colours, overlay area and library sort order.

This data is stored in the app's private storage. It is not uploaded by the developer. Android system backup is disabled for this app, so scripts are not copied to Google Drive by Auto Backup.

03

Data we do not collect

#

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, files, photos, messages, precise location, analytics events or crash logs. Script text is never sent to an ad network.

04

Advertising (Google AdMob)

#

The library and settings screens display banner ads, and an interstitial may appear when you explicitly leave the prompter stage. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google may process:

  • Advertising ID (a resettable identifier),
  • IP address and IP-derived approximate location,
  • Device and app information (model, OS version, app version, language).

This data is shared with Google for advertising and marketing purposes. See Google's How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. No ads are requested in the editor, while scripts are being saved, or during prompter, picture-in-picture and transparent-overlay sessions.

05

Legal basis and consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland)

#

Where required, personalized advertising is based on your consent, collected through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time in the app: Settings → About → Ad privacy options. Non-personalized ads may be served where consent is not required or not granted, in line with Google's policies.

06

US state privacy rights

#

We do not sell personal information for money. Advertising identifiers may be shared with Google for advertising as described above. You can limit this through the in-app Ad privacy options control and by resetting or deleting your advertising ID in Android settings.

07

Screen capture (adaptive contrast)

#

When you enable adaptive contrast, Android screen capture is used solely to measure the average brightness of the screen about once per second so the script text can switch between light and dark colours. Frames are analysed in memory on your device, are never saved, and are never transmitted. Capture stops when you stop the overlay, and a persistent notification with a Stop capture action is shown while it is active.

08

Display over other apps

#

The transparent overlay uses Android's "display over other apps" permission so the script can float above the camera or another app. This permission only lets the app draw its own window; it does not grant access to other apps' content. Touches outside the floating control bar pass through to the app underneath.

09

Notifications

#

A foreground-service notification is shown while the overlay is active, with controls to pause, stop capture or stop the overlay. Notification permission is requested only when you start the overlay.

10

Backup, export and sharing

#

Backup and export files are written only to locations you choose through the system file picker. When you share a script, a temporary copy may be written to the app's cache so the receiving app can read it; these temporary copies are cleared the next time the app starts. Android system backup is disabled.

11

Third-party services

#

Google AdMob and Google Play services (Google LLC) process advertising data as described above. No other third-party SDKs are included.

12

Data retention and deletion

#

Scripts remain on your device until you delete them. Deleting a script moves it to an in-app trash that is purged after 30 days. Uninstalling the app removes all app-private data. There is no server-side account or data to delete. For privacy requests, contact Noxedus2008@gmail.com.

13

Children

#

Teleprompter is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app is not part of Google Play's Designed for Families programme.

14

Security

#

App data is kept in private app storage protected by Android's sandbox. Network requests (ads) use HTTPS/TLS; cleartext network traffic is disabled.

15

Changes to this policy

#

We may update this policy when the app changes. The effective date above will be revised and the new version will be published at this URL.
